PRIVACY POLICY

WHOOP MCP Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how WHOOP MCP is designed to handle information when an authorized user accesses their WHOOP data through a compatible AI assistant.

1. About This Project

WHOOP MCP is an independent personal software project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by WHOOP, Inc. or OpenAI.

2. Information We Access

With your authorization, the integration may access data made available through the official WHOOP API and permitted by the scopes you approve. Depending on the enabled features, this may include:

  • WHOOP account and profile information.
  • Sleep records and sleep performance metrics.
  • Recovery scores, heart rate variability (HRV), and resting heart rate.
  • Physiological cycle and Strain information.
  • Workout and activity records.

The integration may also process OAuth authorization codes and access or refresh tokens to establish and maintain authorized access. It does not need your WHOOP password.

3. How Information Is Used

WHOOP data is intended to be retrieved when you request information or analysis. The purpose is to display, summarize, compare, and explain your authorized WHOOP metrics.

The integration is not intended to sell personal data, use it for advertising, or build a permanent database of raw health records.

4. Storage and Retention

This privacy policy website is a static page and does not itself collect or store WHOOP account data.

The MCP service is designed to process requested WHOOP data on demand rather than retain raw health records indefinitely. OAuth tokens may be stored in protected server-side storage when necessary to maintain authorization. Tokens should be retained only as long as needed and deleted when no longer required.

Technical logs, if enabled, should be limited to operational information and should not intentionally contain access tokens, refresh tokens, or raw health records.

5. Sharing and Third-Party Services

To provide the requested functionality, authorized data may be retrieved from WHOOP and returned to the connected AI assistant as part of a tool response. The assistant provider may process that information under its own applicable privacy policies, terms, and account settings.

WHOOP processes information according to its own policies. The hosting provider may also process technical information required to operate the service.

6. Security

The integration is intended to use HTTPS, request only necessary permissions, and protect OAuth credentials using server-side secret storage. Credentials should never be published in source code, exposed in tool responses, or intentionally written to application logs.

No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Choices

You can stop using the integration and revoke its authorization through the applicable WHOOP account or authorization controls. You may also contact the project maintainer to request deletion of any OAuth credentials retained by the service.

8. Health Disclaimer

WHOOP MCP is an informational tool, not a medical device or a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Its summaries and interpretations may be incomplete or inaccurate.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the integration's functionality, hosting, or data handling practices change. The effective date above will be revised when appropriate.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the project maintainer through the project repository . Do not post health information, passwords, or OAuth tokens in public issues.
